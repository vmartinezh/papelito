# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with no build step and no dependencies. Hosted on GitHub Pages from the public repository `vmartinezh/papelito`. A `.cl` domain may come later if the store does well.

## Users

People in Santiago, Chile, who arrive mostly from Instagram (@papelito.br), often on a phone, looking for stationery, a gift, party favours, an educational game or painting supplies. They want to see what is for sale, choose, and order without friction.

## Product Purpose

Online store for Papelito, a creative stationery project. It sells creative stationery, gifts, keepsakes for celebrations, educational games and painting supplies. Success means a visitor can see the products calmly, build a cart, and send a complete order by WhatsApp.

## Positioning

The opposite of Temu and marketplace-style sites: no ads, no pop-ups, no countdowns, and no information or products competing from every side. The products are the page.

## Operating Context

Orders close on WhatsApp and are paid by bank transfer. There is no payment gateway in this first stage. Customers choose between pickup and delivery, and the details and any delivery cost are confirmed in the chat.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalogue, category browsing, product view and a cart persisted in the browser.
- Checkout builds a pre-written WhatsApp message containing the order.
- The owner must be able to edit products without touching the page code.
- No server: GitHub Pages only serves static files.
- A payment gateway is a possible later stage. It would need a small server function and carries a per-sale commission.
- Undecided: pickup location, delivery areas and costs, real products, photos and prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Papelito. Instagram: @papelito.br. Location: Santiago, Chile.
- Logo: pastel multicolour letters on white with scissors and a flower, and "brasileño" in script. The colours of the site derive from this logo.
- Language: Chilean Spanish.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo: 1600x1600 px original at `docs/img/logo_original.jpeg`.
- No real products, prices, customers or testimonials yet. Sample products must be labelled as samples, and no claims, reviews or delivery promises may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The product comes first: every element that is not a product or a step toward buying must justify itself.
2. No pressure: no urgency tactics, pop-ups or promotional noise.
3. Plain ordering: anyone who can use WhatsApp can complete a purchase.
4. Easy to run: one person can keep the catalogue current without help.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Phone first. Pastel brand colours must not be used for text unless they meet contrast requirements. Full keyboard operation of the cart.
